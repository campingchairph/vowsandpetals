# Vows & Petals — Supplier Outreach Project Brief

## What is Vows & Petals?

Vows & Petals (`https://campingchairph.github.io/vowsandpetals/`) is a free, no-download wedding planning app built for Filipino couples. It helps them manage their guest list, budget, seating chart, vendors, checklist, and wedding program — all in one place, on their phone.

The app has a **Supplier Marketplace** tab where couples browse real, registered wedding businesses by category. When a couple finds a supplier they like, they can view photos, read reviews, and contact the supplier directly (call, Viber, SMS, Messenger, Instagram, Facebook, or website). They can also save the supplier to their own vendor list inside the app.

There is a **separate Supplier Portal** at `https://campingchairph.github.io/vowsandpetals/supplier.html` where business owners register and manage their listing.

---

## Why We Need Suppliers

The app is only as useful as the directory inside it. A couple planning their wedding opens the Supplier tab expecting to find photographers, caterers, florists, and more — **if the directory is empty, the app feels incomplete and couples leave.**

Suppliers are the content. They are what makes couples come back, share the app, and trust it as a serious planning tool.

Beyond filling the directory, suppliers benefit the app in another way: **they are invested in getting couples to use it.** A listed florist will tell their inquiring clients "check out Vows & Petals, I'm on there." That's organic growth we can't buy.

## What Suppliers Get (Free Tier)

- A public listing profile with business name, category, location, and description
- 1 cover photo + 2 gallery photos
- Price range display
- Direct contact links (phone, Viber, SMS, Messenger, Facebook, Instagram, website)
- Star ratings and written reviews from real couples
- Visible to all couples browsing the marketplace — no login required to view
- **Free. No credit card. No setup fee. Takes 2 minutes.**

## What Suppliers Get (Pro Tier — ₱299/mo or ₱2,499/yr)

Everything in Free, plus:
- Up to 9 gallery photos
- A second business listing under the same account
- **Featured** badge that ranks the listing above free listings
- Priority placement in category results

## What Suppliers Get (Verified Badge)

The Verified badge is separate from Pro and is manually granted. It signals trust to couples. Requirements:
- Must have proof of legitimacy (business permit, social media presence, portfolio)
- Must have 10+ "Legit" tags from real clients
- Must have a 4.0+ average star rating

## What Makes a "Legit" Supplier

When doing outreach, prioritize suppliers who have at least one of these:

- **Active social media** — a Facebook page or Instagram with real work posted (not just reposted memes), with engagement from real people
- **Portfolio website** — any domain or even a Carrd/Wix site with photos
- **Reviews on other platforms** — Google Maps, WeddingWire, Kasal.com, or Facebook reviews from named clients
- **Referrals from other suppliers** — caterers know photographers, photographers know coordinators
- **Physical presence** — a shop, studio, or office address listed anywhere

Red flags to avoid:
- No photos of actual work (only stock photos or graphics)
- Social pages created recently with no engagement
- No traceable contact info
- Vague business name with no consistent branding

## Where to Find Suppliers Online

### Facebook
- Search Groups: "Wedding Suppliers Philippines", "Metro Manila Wedding Suppliers", "Cebu Wedding Suppliers", "Davao Wedding Suppliers", etc.
- Search Pages: filter by "Wedding Service", "Photographer", "Caterer" + location
- Look for suppliers who post real work, not just ads

### Instagram
- Hashtags: `#philippinewedding`, `#weddingphilippines`, `#cebuphotogrpaher`, `#manilaweddingphotographer`, `#kasalph`, `#pilipinaskasal`
- Search the hashtag for the category + location: `#bulacanweddingvenue`, `#davaobridal`, etc.
- Suppliers who tag their city are easiest to verify as local

### TikTok
- Suppliers who post wedding BTS content are highly active and social-media-savvy
- Search: "wedding supplier Philippines", "same day edit Philippines", "bridal glam transformation Philippines"
- These suppliers are already comfortable with online platforms and easier to onboard

### Google Maps
- Search "wedding photographer [city]", "wedding caterer [city]"
- Suppliers with Google Business Profiles are more established
- Prioritize those with 10+ reviews

### Kasal.com / WeddingWire PH / Bridestory
- Suppliers already listed on these platforms are pre-vetted and used to directory listings
- Approach: "We're a free, newer platform building a Filipino-first supplier directory"

### Referrals
- Ask one confirmed supplier to introduce you to their network
- "Do you know a good florist / photographer / coordinator I should reach out to?"
- Supplier networks are tight — one warm intro is worth 10 cold DMs

## Outreach Tone and Approach

### Who We Are Talking To
Filipino small-business owners. Most are sole proprietors or family businesses. They run their own Facebook page, reply to their own DMs, and juggle bookings manually. They are:
- Busy
- Skeptical of "free" offers that turn into fees later
- Not very tech-savvy in most cases
- Highly responsive to personal, warm messages over copy-paste blasts
- Moved by stories and community ("help other Filipino couples")

### Core Message
> "We're building a free directory for Filipino wedding suppliers. You get a profile, photo gallery, and a direct link for couples to contact you — for free, forever on the basic plan. It takes 2 minutes to register."

### What NOT to Say
- Don't lead with "Pro plan" or pricing — that comes after they're in
- Don't say "sign up" — say "register your business" or "get listed"
- Don't use corporate/formal language — conversational Tagalog-English (Taglish) works better for DMs
- Don't promise traffic numbers we can't back up (the app is new)

### Sample DM (English)
> Hi [Name / Business Name]! 👋 I came across your work on Instagram and I love your style.
>
> I'm building Vows & Petals — a free wedding planning app for Filipino couples. We have a supplier directory where couples browse and contact vendors directly (call, Viber, Messenger — whichever you prefer).
>
> Getting listed is completely free and takes about 2 minutes. No fees, no commitments. Couples searching for [their category] in [their city] will be able to find you through the app.
>
> Would you like me to send you the link to register? 🌸

### Sample DM (Taglish)
> Hi po! 👋 Nakita ko ang inyong work at napaganda po ng output ninyo!
>
> Gumagawa kami ng libre na wedding planning app para sa mga Filipino couples — Vows & Petals. May supplier directory kami doon kung saan pwedeng mag-browse ang mga couples at makipag-contact sa inyo directly (call, Viber, Messenger).
>
> Libre pong mag-register — 2 minutes lang. Walang bayad, walang commitment. Tara na po? 🌸

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "May bayad ba?" | "Libre po ang basic listing — photos, reviews, contact links. May Pro option din po para sa mas maraming photos at featured placement, pero hindi required." |
| "Bago pa lang yung app, may users na ba?" | "Oo, bago pa lang kami pero aktibo na kaming nagtatayo ng directory. Ang advantage — mas madaling ma-rank at makita ang inyong listing ngayon kaysa later kapag marami na." |
| "May Kasal.com na ako / Bridestory na ako" | "Alam po namin — at doon mismo kami naghahanap ng magagandang suppliers. Libre lang po ito, isang additional platform lang. Hindi kayo nagbabayad ng anything." |
| "Busy ako" | "2 minutes lang po talaga — business name, category, location, photo, contact. Yan na lahat." |
| "Paano ko malalaman kung may nakita sa amin?" | "Makikita ninyo ang inyong public profile at pwede rin kayong mag-upload ng photos kahit kailan. Reviews din po ay mapapanood nyo." |

## Tracking Outreach

When reaching out, track these per supplier:
- **Platform found** (IG, Facebook, TikTok, Google, referral)
- **Category**
- **Location / city**
- **Date contacted**
- **Response** (replied / no reply / registered / not interested)
- **Notes** (what they said, follow-up needed)

A simple spreadsheet or Notion table works. The goal is to avoid messaging the same supplier twice and to know which platforms/categories are converting.
