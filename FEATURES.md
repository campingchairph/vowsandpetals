# Vows & Petals — Full Feature List
### Wedding Planner App · www.vows-and-petals.com

> Free, no-download, works on any device. Built for Philippine couples planning their dream wedding.

---

## 🏠 Overview Dashboard

- **Couple names, wedding date, venue, and budget** — set once, shown everywhere
- **Live countdown** to the wedding day (days and months)
- **4-stat summary card** — days to go, guest count, total committed, planning percentage
- **Progress bar** — shows how much of the checklist is done
- **Budget snapshot** — total budget, committed amount, remaining balance (or over-budget warning)
- **Edit details button** — update couple info anytime

---

## 👥 Guest List & RSVP Management

- **Add unlimited guests** — name, group/entourage, phone number, meal choice, dietary restrictions
- **RSVP tracking** — Attending ✅ / Pending ⏳ / Declined ❌ per guest
- **Guest groups** — organize guests by entourage (Bride's Side, Groom's Side, etc.)
- **Collapsible groups** — keep the list clean and manageable
- **Search** — find any guest instantly by name or group
- **Filter by status** — view all, attending, pending, declined, or invite-not-sent
- **Guest count badge** — live total per group with attending count
- **💌 Send personal invite** — each guest gets their own unique RSVP link via share sheet or clipboard
- **🔗 Copy RSVP link** — copy a direct link to share anywhere
- **RSVP sync** — pull latest responses from cloud with one tap
- **Meal tracking** — chicken / fish / vegetarian per guest
- **Dietary notes** — free-text dietary restrictions per guest
- **Edit & remove** — update any guest detail anytime
- **Invite sent tracking** — marks which guests have already been invited with the date sent

---

## 💰 Budget Tracker

- **Set total wedding budget** — in Philippine Peso
- **Add expenses** — name, amount, category (venue, catering, flowers, etc.)
- **Mark as paid / unpaid** — track what's settled vs outstanding
- **Real-time totals** — committed amount updates instantly
- **Remaining balance** — always visible, turns red when over budget
- **Category breakdown** — see spending by category
- **Edit and delete** — full control over every line item
- **Budget warning** — visual alert when expenses exceed budget

---

## ✅ Wedding Checklist

- **Auto-generated timeline** — choose 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, or 24 months to the wedding
- **Phase-based planning** — tasks organized by planning phase (12 months out, 6 months out, etc.)
- **31+ curated tasks** — covers booking vendors, sending invites, final fittings, and day-of logistics
- **Check off tasks** — tap to mark complete
- **Overall progress bar** — see your planning percentage at a glance
- **Per-phase progress** — done/total count on each phase header
- **Collapsible phases** — hide completed phases to focus on what's next
- **Change timeline** — adjust your planning window without losing progress
- **Reset checklist** — start over if plans change

---

## 🪑 Seating Canvas

- **Drag-and-drop floorplan** — build your venue layout visually
- **Furniture palette** — add round tables, rectangular tables, long tables, and chairs
- **Pan and zoom** — navigate large venue layouts easily
- **Rotate furniture** — orient tables and chairs to match your venue
- **Assign guests to seats** — link each guest to a specific chair
- **Visual seat status** — see which seats are filled and which are empty
- **Guest seat labels** — names shown on the canvas
- **Delete furniture** — remove and rearrange freely
- **Auto-chair generation** — adding a table automatically adds chairs around it
- **Entourage section** — separate layout area for principal sponsors and entourage

---

## 📅 Day Program / Schedule

- **Build your wedding day timeline** — event by event
- **Add time slots** — ceremony, reception, cocktail hour, first dance, etc.
- **Custom event names** — fully customizable labels
- **Reorder events** — drag to rearrange the timeline
- **Add notes per event** — attach details to each schedule item
- **Edit and delete** — adjust the program as plans evolve
- **Month and week calendar views** — see the bigger planning picture
- **Countdown integration** — timeline tied to your wedding date

---

## 💌 Invitation Card Builder

- **Auto-generated invitation card** — 3-page card built from your couple info
  - Page 1: Main invitation (names, date, venue)
  - Page 2: Program / details
  - Page 3: RSVP / map
- **Custom card background** — upload your own invitation design *(coming soon)*
- **QR code** — auto-generated RSVP QR linking to your personal RSVP page
- **Zoom to full screen** — tap any card page to view at full size
- **Long-press to save** — save card images directly from mobile
- **Personalized invites** — each guest's invite link is pre-filled with their name
- **Share invite** — send via native share sheet, messaging apps, or clipboard copy

---

## 🌐 RSVP Page (Guest-Facing)

- **Standalone RSVP page** — works on any phone, no app needed for guests
- **Pre-filled guest name** — link arrives with the guest's name already filled in
- **RSVP options** — Attending / Declining
- **Meal choice selection** — chicken / fish / vegetarian
- **Message to couple** — free-text field for notes
- **Instant cloud submission** — response saved immediately to Firestore
- **Auto-sync to planner** — couple sees updated RSVP status with one tap

---

## ☁️ Cloud Sync & Save

- **Auto-save to localStorage** — every change saved instantly, works offline
- **Cloud sync via Firebase** — sign in to back up and sync across devices
- **Google sign-in** — one-tap login with Google account
- **Email & password** — traditional sign-in option
- **Conflict resolution** — if local and cloud data differ, choose which to keep
- **Cloud save indicator** — shows sync status in the header
- **Load from cloud** — restore your plan on any new device

## 📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)

- **No download required** — runs in any browser
- **Works offline** — full planning functionality without internet
- **Installable** — add to home screen on iPhone and Android
- **No account required** — start planning immediately, sign in later to back up
- **Mobile-first design** — built for phones, also great on tablet and desktop
- **Fast** — no app store, no updates to download

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

- **Your data is yours** — stored in your browser and your personal Firebase account
- **No one else can see your data** — Firestore rules lock data per user
- **Sign out anytime** — data stays in your browser even when signed out
- **Free forever** — the core planner is and always will be free
